About World Disaster Dashboard

A free, real-time global crisis monitor that places natural and humanitarian disaster data on a live 3D rotating Earth globe. Made to watch simply by eyes.

Key Features

True 3D rotating Earth globe
Multiple Earth textures
Auto-refresh every 30 seconds
Type, severity & time filters
New-disaster toasts + sound
Live news in point popups
Live stats + chart
Dark / Light modes
Offline cache + demo data
Fully responsive

Data Sources

  • EMSC / CSEM – European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre
  • USGS – U.S. Geological Survey earthquake feeds
  • GDACS – Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (UN/EC)
  • NASA EONET – Earth Observatory Natural Event Tracker
  • Volcanoes – Smithsonian / NOAA global volcano catalog + GDACS & EONET eruptions
  • freenewsapi.ai – Keyless news search

Globe Textures

  • Basic – NASA Blue Marble
  • Satellite – Earth at night / city lights
  • Dark Map – Dark Earth
  • Greenlines – Topographic / terrain
